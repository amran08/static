    Dhaka Rickshaw Run

    Salaam,

    You drive a Bangla Tesla down Mirpur Road at rush hour. Dodge traffic, collect batteries!

    You get 5 lives ❤❤❤❤❤ per run. Crash or run flat and you respawn right where you were, distance kept, with a 2-second shield. Watch for people crossing: hit one and you lose 100 m.

    Why "Tesla"? Dhaka nicknamed its battery rickshaws after the electric car brand: they run on batteries, and they're the poor man's EV.
